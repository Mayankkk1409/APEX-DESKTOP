Project APEX - Documentation & User Flow  
 
Executive Summary: 
 
Project APEX or APEX trading terminal is a fully automated AI powered 
option trading platform, which is completely hassle free for a trader as they 
only need to enter the ticker of any U.S. based company, select the 
chart-timeframe and adjust the view of the interactive technical chart, select 
the expiry date options from the dropdown, and then hit SCAN. After hitting 
scan the APEX AI will run a deep synthesized analysis consisting of: 
 
●​ A 10 layer deep technical analysis(MACD, EMA, RSI, BOLLINGER 
BANDS, PIVOT POINTS, SUPPORT/RESISTANCE, SUPERTREND, 
VOLUME, TECHNICAL CANDLESTICK PATTERNS) 
●​ Upon running a 10 layer deep technical analysis on the charts the AI 
will start analyzing the entire option chain based on the user selected 
expiry dates. This part of the analysis mainly checks and looks at the 
greeks (DELTA, THETA, GAMMA,VEGA) as well as the bid/ask 
spread,volume, change, and open interest(OI) to locate a proper set 
up.  
●​ Next it will compare implied volatility (IV) with historical volatility(HV) 
to determine the ideal scenario of buying or selling the contract.  
●​ Next the system will scan and analyze for live real time sentimental 
thesis from financial news and other reliable sources as well the 
technical charts and option charts to understand investor sentiment.  
●​ The system will also pull a fundamental snapshot of the company 
such as the earnings per share(EPS), revenue, price/earnings 
ratio(P/E), sales, income, analysts estimates, growth 
rates/expectation to run a scan that will determine the health of the 
company.  
●​ After collecting all the information mentioned above the individual 
layers of information get synthesized from all the layers and runs a 
deep analysis and research model that will give a rating/score.

The main innovation of the APEX is the “APEX STRATEGY”. 
 
We have designed this strategy to capitalize once volatility sparks, before 
and after a major catalyst event, and change in sentiments.  
 
The strategy works in 2 sorts of mechanics one being where you buy and 
one where you sell the option.  
 
At first you are given a major upcoming event related to a specific company 
or the broader market, while leading up to that event you are going to do 
the 2 mechanics we discussed for the strategy to work in the intended 
manner.  
 
While leading up to the event you buy a call option and a put option both 
OTM and both expiring a week after the event.  
 
●​ You want to buy OTM as it is much cheaper and allows for much 
more leverage. After the event the stock can potentially make large 
moves in both directions and the crashing of volatility will allow for the 
trade to work in your favor.  
●​ The time frame should be 1 week as it is much more optimal because 
it is not too far out and too far in giving enough time for the market to 
price in.

EMA (Exponential Moving Averages) 
 
For technical analysis, trend confirmation, & price action set up we focus on EMA 
Stacks of (9, 21, 50, 100, 200) ranging from both immediate short term to mid-long term.

Bullish​
When the price is constantly above & sustaining above each level of the EMA Stack 
starting from short/immediate (9) to mid/long (200) showing a consistent & strong 
uptrend. The best & classic entry point is when it touches the 9-21 EMA & sustaining 
above them as well as crosses those levels. The crossover of a shorter term moving

●​ Golden Crossover (50 EMA crosses 200 EMA from bottom) 
●​ When the price is unusually below the moving averages it often sets up an

Bearish​
When the price is constantly below the EMA Stack, again starting from short/immediate 
(9) to mid/long (200). The classic & strongest entry point would be the pullback entry — 
when the price goes below the EMA, rises back near to touch the EMA line, and once it 
is near that line look for reversal signals in candlestick patterns & enter bearish trade. 
The crossover of a short term moving average to long term average from above. 
●​ Death Crossover (50 EMA crosses 200 EMA from above) 
●​ When price is unusually above EMA it suggests a reversal 
The EMA number changes based on the timeframe used by the trader. For intraday & 
day trading the 9-21 EMAs are most applicable, for standard swing trading 12-26 EMA, 
for longer swings the 50-200 EMA. On a little longer term vision the 50-200 EMAs are 
highly effective.

Bollinger Bands 
 
The Bollinger Bands consist of 3 lines: 
●​ A middle simple moving average (20 periods) 
●​ The other 2 are upper resistance level & lower support level which is a standard 
deviation of usually 2 away from middle 
Bullish​
When the price action (candlestick) crosses the middle line, sustains above it and 
breaks out by crossing the upper band & breaking out suggesting a potential uptrend. 
The breakout price level acts as a support line. 
Bearish​
When the price action (candlestick) goes below the midline, sustains below it and 
breaks down below by crossing the lower band suggesting a downtrend. The 
breakdown level acts as the resistance level. 
Bullish (W-Pattern)​
The price action (candlestick) breaks below the midline & slides near or even beyond 
the lower bound but it retreats back to the midline & again pulls back to the earlier 
support level & then shows an uptrend again by breaking out above midline making it an 
ideal entry point. 
Bearish (M-Pattern)​
The price action (candlestick) breaks above the upper band or gets near that level & 
pulls back to the midline, gets a support at the midline & bounces back to the earlier 
resistance to again pull back & this time break the midline showing signs of a potential 
downtrend with the midline being the resistance — marking the breaking of the midline 
a perfect entry to enter a bearish trade.

Pivot Points 
 
Standard Formula: (High + Low + Close) / 3 
●​ R1: (PP x 2) - Low; R2: PP + (High - Low); R3: High + 2 x (PP - Low) 
●​ S1: (PP x 2) - High; S2: PP - (High - Low); S3: Low - 2 x (High - PP) 
Pivot points are historical horizontal price levels calculated from previous day's highs & 
lows & close, projecting key future levels. 
Bullish​
When the price is above the central pivot level the upward trend is continued with the 
support level being at pivot & targets expanding outward to R1, R2, & R3. 
Bearish​
When the price is below the central pivot level the downward trend is continued with 
resistance level being at pivot & downward targets expanding to S1, S2, & S3. 
 
 
Support & Resistance 
 
These are structural price levels where the stock price has historically stopped or 
reversed from, which based on supply & demand scenarios. 
Bullish​
When the stock price breaks above the resistance level (price level where trend 
reverses) suggesting a potential upside. 
Bearish​
When the stock price breaks below the support level (price level stock price doesn't 
break) suggesting a bearish trend. 
When the support or resistance is broken but the price again retreats to those levels in 
short span it shows a false breakout & potentially more buying/selling pressure.

Volume 
 
Volume represents the number of shares, contracts, or tokens of an underlying asset 
that are traded on a daily basis. 
Bullish​
When the price is constantly in a bullish trend making higher highs & the volume is also 
increasing but if the stock sees a dip so does the volume, indicating that there is a

Bearish​
When the price is constantly in a bearish run making lower lows & the volume decides 
to follow & if the stock trend reverses so does volume but not by a huge amount

Bullish Divergence​
The price is making lower lows but volume is making higher lows showing high demand 
& buying at low levels suggesting reversal. 
Bearish Divergence​
The price is making higher highs but volume is at lower highs showing selling pressure 
suggesting reversal from current trend.
