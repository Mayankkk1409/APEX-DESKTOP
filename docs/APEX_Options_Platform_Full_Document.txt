APEX is a professional-grade, fully autonomous AI options trading platform. A trader types any
U.S. equity ticker, selects a chart timeframe and options expiry from a dropdown, hits SCAN —
and within 15 seconds the platform has:
1. Run a 10-layer technical analysis on the price chart (MACD, RSI, EMA stack, SuperTrend,
Bollinger Bands, Candlestick Patterns, Pivot Points, Support/Resistance)
2. Loaded the live options chain and filtered every strike by Delta, Theta, Vega, Gamma, and
bid/ask spread
3. Compared Implied Volatility (IV) to Historical Volatility (HV) to determine whether to buy
or sell premium
4. Processed real-time sentiment from financial news and options flow
5. Pulled a fundamental snapshot: earnings history, EPS trend, analyst consensus, revenue
growth
6. Synthesized all five layers into a single APEX Composite Score (0–100)
7. Selected the optimal strategy from its approved playbook (Iron Condor, Bull Call Spread,
Bear Put Spread, Gamma Trampoline™, and more)
8. Presented a full trade brief in plain English — and executed autonomously when the score
clears the 72/100 threshold
APEX is not a signal dashboard. It is a full-cycle AI trading co-pilot that thinks like an expert
options desk, executes with institutional discipline, and explains every decision in plain
language.
The platform's proprietary innovation is the Gamma Trampoline™ — an original options
strategy that exploits IV inversion around catalyst events (earnings, product launches) to
dramatically reduce the cost of a standard straddle while maintaining full long-gamma upside.

10.1 Origin & Design Philosophy


===== PAGE 13/23 =====

1. Subsidizes the cost of the long back-week options by selling front-week volatility at its peak
2. Retains full long-gamma participation after the catalyst event expires
3. Provides meaningful downside protection if the stock moves flat
                    [ CATALYST EVENT (e.g., Earnings) ]
     Front-Week Expiry                   Back-Week Expiry
   (Day After Catalyst)               (Two Weeks Post-Catalyst)
   ─────────────────────              ─────────────────────────
   SELL OTM Call [Strike A]           BUY OTM Call [Strike A]
   SELL OTM Put  [Strike B]           BUY OTM Put  [Strike B]
   (Same strikes, different expirations — the key innovation)
Step 1 — Buy the Back-Week Long Options
Buy 1 OTM Call expiring two weeks after the catalyst at Strike A (above current price)
Buy 1 OTM Put expiring two weeks after the catalyst at Strike B (below current price)
These are your long-gamma anchors; they retain substantial time value through and
beyond the event
Step 2 — Sell the Front-Week Short Options
Sell 1 OTM Call expiring the day after the catalyst at the same Strike A
Sell 1 OTM Put expiring the day after the catalyst at the same Strike B
These exploit the IV spike that panicked traders create before the event
Net Effect: Premium from front-week shorts heavily subsidizes back-week longs (typically 50–
70% offset), resulting in a net cost far below a conventional straddle while maintaining full
long-gamma participation through the back week.
Scenario A: Large Post-Earnings Move (The Trampoline Fires)
Front-week short option is breached but expires the next day with minimal remaining time
value
Back-week long option (now deep ITM with a full week of time value) surges
The stock "bounces" off the short strike and catapults the long option's profit
The trade profits because long option value growth outpaces short option loss
Scenario B: Flat Earnings
Both front-week options expire worthless → full premium collected
Back-week options now held at dramatically reduced cost
